# Third-party attribution: EXPINT

The Padé recurrence in `src/phi.jl` is a Julia port of EXPINT's
`common/phipade.m`. Exponential-method coefficient formulae also draw on EXPINT
and the numerical-analysis references cited in the solver documentation.

EXPINT 1.1 is attributed to Håvard Berland, Bård Skaflestad, and Will Wright.
Its README is dated 23 November 2005.
